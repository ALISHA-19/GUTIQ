The riskiest question

Will people pay for GutIQ without talking to a human?

If yes, GutIQ scales like software. If not, growth stops where your calendar stops. My work comes down to two things: send human time only to the leads that need it, and automate the rest.

THE INSIGHT

Low prices suit viral and content loops; high prices suit sales-led loops. The app sits close to the risky middle. GutIQ's advantage is that free content feeds high-ticket PURO, so the hand-off from content to a consult has to be fast. That is where lead scoring comes in.
